Power of attorney for Turkey from the UK: how to sign it

Dr Alihan Sağır, Turkish lawyer (Avukat), Antalya Bar Association · Prepared: 20 November 2026

You can sell a flat, settle an inheritance, run a company or bring a court case in Turkey without travelling, if someone in Turkey holds a valid Turkish power of attorney (vekaletname). From the UK you sign it at a Turkish consulate, or before a UK notary public with an apostille and a Turkish translation. This guide covers both routes, the wording and revocation.

In short

  1. A Turkish power of attorney is a written authority for another person to act for you. Turkish offices accept only the acts expressly written in it.
  2. There are two routes from the UK: sign at a Turkish consulate, which drafts the document in Turkish, or sign before a UK notary public, have the document apostilled by the FCDO Legalisation Office, translated by a sworn translator and the translation certified by a Turkish notary.
  3. A power of attorney signed at a Turkish consulate needs no apostille or written translation, because it is a Turkish document drawn up in Turkish. If you do not speak Turkish, a sworn translator interprets it orally at the signing. A UK notary document does, and the apostille only proves the notary's signature and status.
  4. Name the powers and the object: the property, the right to sell, the price. For court, each special authority in Code of Civil Procedure Art. 74.
  5. Only a lawyer can represent you in court on your behalf (Attorneys' Act Art. 35). In court or legal matters, a relative or any other third party who is not a registered lawyer cannot act for you. A relative can still hold a power of attorney for the land registry or tax office.
  6. No statutory expiry. The authority runs until it is revoked, the task ends, or a time limit written into the document passes.
  7. A UK Lasting Power of Attorney is a different instrument. The practical route for Turkey is a Turkish-format document.

What a Turkish power of attorney is

A vekaletname is the written proof that you authorised another person, the attorney (vekil), to act in your name. The underlying agreement is a mandate under the Code of Obligations (No. 6098, Art. 502): the attorney undertakes to do a task for the grantor. The acts the attorney performs within the authority bind you directly (Art. 40).

A general power of attorney covers administration without listing each act. A special one names the transaction, the object and the powers. Title deed offices, tax offices and courts ask for the special kind.

Examples of wording that offices expect:

  • Sale of a property: the province, district, neighbourhood, block and parcel numbers, the authority to sell, to agree the price and to receive it.
  • Inheritance: to apply to the civil court of peace for a certificate of inheritance, to file the inheritance tax return, to register the heirs and, if intended, to sell. See Inherited property in Turkey and Inheritance tax in Turkey.
  • Company: to sign the articles, register the company, open and run a bank account. See Company formation in Türkiye.
  • Court: the lawyer's litigation power of attorney (avukatlık vekaletnamesi). Under Code of Civil Procedure Art. 74 a lawyer cannot, unless expressly authorised, settle the case, challenge the judge, amend the whole claim, offer or accept an oath, waive the claim or a right of appeal, accept the other side's claim, release the other party, lift an attachment, agree to arbitration or apply to alternative dispute resolution. A court power of attorney lists these acts one by one.

Route A: sign at a Turkish consulate

A Turkish consulate prepares the power of attorney in Turkish and certifies it itself. A Turkish office treats a document issued or approved by a Turkish embassy or consulate as a Turkish document. No apostille and no written translation are needed. The land registry's circular states that where the foreign country's own rules cannot meet the Turkish form requirements, the power of attorney may be issued by a Turkish consulate.

The Consulate General in London takes notary appointments online through its website. Each consulate sets its own rules on which acts it takes from non-Turkish nationals, so read its notary page before booking. Your lawyer in Turkey sends you the Turkish text to bring.

If you do not speak Turkish, a sworn translator must attend the signing and translate the document to you orally. This is interpretation at the appointment, not the written translation of the notary route. Turkish consulates publish lists of sworn translators: contact a translator from your consulate's list and arrange for them to attend before you book the appointment.

The original does not need to be posted to Turkey if the power of attorney authorises the attorney to obtain copies through the Union of Turkish Notaries system (TNB). The attorney can then get a certified copy from any notary in Turkey for a small fee. Ask the consulate to include this authority when the text is drawn up.

Route B: UK notary, apostille, translation

  1. Sign before a notary public in the UK, with the Turkish-format text agreed in advance. A notary or solicitor who certifies the document is a "public official" whose documents the FCDO can legalise, and powers of attorney are listed on gov.uk as an example.
  2. Apostille. The FCDO Legalisation Office checks the notary's signature and seal against its records and attaches the apostille. The UK and Turkey are both parties to the 1961 Hague Apostille Convention, so no consular legalisation follows. The apostille certifies the signature and capacity of the signer of the document, not its content.
  3. Translation. The translation into Turkish can be done in the UK or in Turkey. In Turkey it usually costs less, but the apostilled original has to be sent there. A sworn translator (yeminli tercüman) translates the document and the apostille, and a Turkish notary certifies the translation.

For a property sale or an inheritance, the consulate is the shorter route. The UK notary route suits a grantor who cannot reach a consulate.

Title deed offices and the wording

The land registry (tapu müdürlüğü) rejects a document that does not name the property and the act. For a flat in Kaş, Kalkan, Fethiye or Alanya, the text gives the parcel details and says whether the attorney may sell, at what price and whether the attorney may collect the money. A document that says only "manage my property in Turkey" means signing a new one.

Who can be your attorney

Anyone with legal capacity can be named: a relative, a friend or a lawyer. For property and tax matters a relative can act. In court the position is different: Attorneys' Act Art. 35 reserves representing others before courts, and conducting their cases, to lawyers registered with a bar. A party may act in their own case. In court or legal matters, a relative or any other third party who is not a registered lawyer cannot act for you, even with a power of attorney.

The attorney must act within the authority given and in your interest.

Validity and revocation

A Turkish power of attorney has no statutory expiry. It can also be issued for a fixed period, and then it ends when that period expires.

Revocation. You may end the mandate at any time (Code of Obligations Art. 512, and Art. 42 for the authority). If you end it at an unsuitable time, you owe the attorney compensation for the loss it causes. Revoke in writing before a Turkish notary or consulate by a notarised revocation (azilname) and have it served on the attorney. Tell the offices that hold the document, such as the land registry and the tax office: under Art. 42 you cannot rely on the revocation against a third party acting in good faith who was not told.

Death. Unless the document or the nature of the task says otherwise, the mandate ends with the death of the grantor or the attorney (Art. 513). If ending it would endanger the grantor's interests, the attorney or the attorney's heirs must carry on until the heirs can act (Art. 513, para. 2). The heirs then need their own power of attorney.

A UK Lasting Power of Attorney

A UK Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA) is a UK instrument for decisions when someone loses mental capacity. It is not a Turkish vekaletname, and whether a Turkish office accepts it is uncertain. For Turkish property or inheritance, sign a Turkish-format power of attorney; for LPA questions, a UK solicitor advises on UK law.

Checklist before you sign

  • Passport, with the name spelled as on the title deed.
  • Property details: province, district, neighbourhood, block and parcel.
  • The attorney's name, ID or passport number and address.
  • The exact acts: sell, price, receive, sign the deed, file the return, represent in court.
  • For the consulate route: a sworn translator from the consulate's list, if you do not speak Turkish, and authority for the attorney to obtain certified copies through the TNB system.
  • Whether the power of attorney should end on a set date.
  • For the notary route: the apostille and the sworn translation, certified by a Turkish notary.

Frequently asked questions

How do I give power of attorney to someone in Turkey from the UK?

Sign a Turkish-format power of attorney at a Turkish consulate in the UK and you're done, or sign it before a UK notary public, then apostille it through the FCDO Legalisation Office, translate it into Turkish with a sworn translator and have a Turkish notary certify the translation.

Can a UK power of attorney be used in Turkey?

A power of attorney signed before a UK notary can be used, if it is apostilled, translated and certified. A UK Lasting Power of Attorney is a different instrument, and Turkish offices are not bound to accept it.

Do I need an apostille if I sign at the Turkish consulate?

No. A document issued by a Turkish consulate is a Turkish document, and Turkish offices accept it without apostille or written translation. If you do not speak Turkish, a sworn translator from the consulate's list interprets it at the signing.

How long is a power of attorney valid in Turkey?

No statute sets an expiry. It stays valid until you revoke it, the task is finished, the grantor or attorney dies, or the time limit written into the text passes.

How do I cancel a power of attorney in Turkey?

Revoke it in writing through a Turkish notary or consulate and serve the revocation on the attorney. Also notify the land registry or other office that holds the document, because a good-faith third party who was not told can rely on the old authority (Code of Obligations Art. 42).

Is there a sample power of attorney for Turkey?

A generic sample does not work. Offices want the property, the act and the powers named, so the Turkish text is drafted for your transaction and agreed with the attorney before you sign.

Can a relative act for me in a Turkish court?

No. In court or legal matters, a relative or any other third party who is not a registered lawyer cannot act for you (Attorneys' Act Art. 35). A relative can hold a power of attorney for the land registry or tax office, but a court case needs a lawyer holding a litigation power of attorney.
