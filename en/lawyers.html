Our lawyers

Nizamettin Sağır

Nizamettin Sağır graduated from Ankara University Faculty of Law in 1987 and established Sağır Hukuk Bürosu in 1988. He has specialised in construction law and zoning law, and also works in expropriation, commercial law and the law of obligations.

Nil Sağır

Nil Sağır graduated from Ankara University Faculty of Law in 1986 and subsequently moved to Antalya, where she began practising law. She has been a partner at Sağır Hukuk Bürosu since 1989. She has specialised in employment law, family law, inheritance law and the acquisition of real estate by foreign nationals. She served as Deputy Chair of the Antalya Bar Association’s Traineeship Board until 2014. From 2009 to 2021, she served as Sweden’s Honorary Consul in Antalya.

Dr. Alihan Sağır

Alihan Sağır began his studies at İhsan Doğramacı Bilkent University Faculty of Law on a full scholarship in 2010 and graduated in 2014. During his studies, he took the International Commercial Litigation and Arbitration course at the London School of Economics in 2013. After graduating, he completed a one-year certificate programme in Construction Contracts Law at Ankara University’s Research Institute of Banking and Commercial Law.

In 2017, he completed his master’s degree with a thesis in Private Law at Ankara University Faculty of Law. His thesis concerned the contractor’s failure to perform its obligations under a contract for work.

Since 2020, he has practised as a mediator registered with the Department of Mediation of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Türkiye.

In 2023, he completed his doctorate in the Department of Private Law at Akdeniz University Faculty of Law. His doctoral thesis concerned the platform contract in crowdfunding systems and its legal consequences.

Tuğçe Sağır

Tuğçe Sağır began her studies at Çankaya University Faculty of Law in 2013 and graduated in 2017. She has worked at Sağır Hukuk Bürosu since 2021.

Having specialised in criminal law, family law and inheritance law, Tuğçe Sağır also works in administrative law and cadastral law.
