Our lawyers

Av. Nizamettin Sağır

Av. Nizamettin Sağır graduated from Ankara University Faculty of Law in 1987 and founded Sağır Hukuk Bürosu in 1988. Registered with the Antalya Bar Association, he practises primarily in construction law and zoning law, as well as expropriation, commercial law and the law of obligations.

Av. Nil Sağır

Av. Nil Sağır graduated from Ankara University Faculty of Law in 1986 and subsequently moved to Antalya, where she began practising law. Since 1989, she has practised at Sağır Hukuk Bürosu. Registered with the Antalya Bar Association, she works in employment law, family law, inheritance law and the acquisition of real estate by foreign nationals. She served as Deputy Chair of the Antalya Bar Association’s Traineeship Board until 2014, and as Sweden’s Honorary Consul in Antalya from 2009 to 2021.

Av. Dr. Alihan Sağır

Av. Dr. Alihan Sağır graduated from İhsan Doğramacı Bilkent University Faculty of Law in 2014. During his undergraduate studies, he completed the International Commercial Litigation and Arbitration course at the London School of Economics in 2013. Following graduation, he completed a one-year certificate programme in Construction Contracts Law at Ankara University’s Research Institute of Banking and Commercial Law.

In 2017, he obtained his LL.M. in Private Law from Ankara University Graduate School of Social Sciences with a thesis on the contractor’s failure to perform its obligations under contracts for work and services (“Eser Sözleşmesinde Yüklenicinin Borcunu İfa Etmemesi”).

In 2023, he obtained his Ph.D. in Private Law from Akdeniz University Institute of Social Sciences with a doctoral dissertation on the platform contract in crowdfunding systems and its legal consequences (“Kitle Fonlaması Sisteminde Platform Sözleşmesi ve Hukukî Sonuçları”).

He is registered with the Antalya Bar Association and, since 2020, with the Official Register of Mediators of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Türkiye. He practises in commercial and corporate law, startup and venture finance law, information technology and artificial intelligence law, and contract law, and speaks fluent English.

Av. Tuğçe Sağır

Av. Tuğçe Sağır began her studies at Çankaya University Faculty of Law in 2013 and graduated in 2017. She has practised at Sağır Hukuk Bürosu since 2021.

Registered with the Antalya Bar Association, she works in criminal law, family law and inheritance law, as well as administrative law and cadastral law.
