About us

Sağır Hukuk Bürosu was founded in Antalya in 1988 by Av. Nizamettin Sağır. The office continues its work from its address in the Muratpaşa district.

Transparency, the rule of law and professional ethics guide the office’s approach. Its legal practice is conducted with regard for the reputation of the profession.

The office works in the law of obligations and contracts, family law, criminal law, employment law, inheritance law, commercial and corporate law, enforcement and insolvency law, administrative law, information technology and artificial intelligence law, startup law and urban regeneration. The Practice Areas page provides further information about the matters covered.

Av. Nizamettin Sağır, Av. Nil Sağır, Av. Dr. Alihan Sağır and Av. Tuğçe Sağır practise at the office. Their education and professional backgrounds are presented on the Lawyers page.

To contact the office or request information, please use the telephone and email details on the Contact page, where you will also find the address and contact form.
