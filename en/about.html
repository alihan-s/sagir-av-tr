About us

Sağır Hukuk Bürosu was founded in Antalya in 1988 by Nizamettin Sağır. The office continues its work from its address in the Muratpaşa district.

Transparency, the rule of law and professional ethics guide the office’s approach. Its legal practice is conducted with regard for the reputation of the profession.

The office works in obligations and contract law, family law, criminal law, employment law, inheritance law, commercial and company law, enforcement and insolvency law, administrative law, startup law and urban transformation. The Practice Areas page provides further information about the matters covered.

Nizamettin Sağır, Nil Sağır, Dr. Alihan Sağır and Tuğçe Sağır practise at the office. Their education and professional backgrounds are presented on the Lawyers page.

To contact the office or request information, please use the telephone and email details on the Contact page, where you will also find the address and contact form.
