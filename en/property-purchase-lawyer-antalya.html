Buying property in Antalya: legal assistance for foreign buyers

Sağır Hukuk Bürosu is a law office in Antalya, founded in 1988. We assist foreign buyers purchasing property across Antalya province, including Alanya, Manavgat, Side and Kemer. You can write to us in English or Turkish. Our office is in Muratpaşa, Antalya city.

Before you pay or sign

We review the title deed record, the building and its permits, and the proposed sale contract before a purchase. The checks depend on whether the property is completed, under construction or being bought from a private owner.

  • Title deed and seller. The registered owner, signing authority, mortgages, seizures and other annotations.
  • Building and permits. The status of the flat, construction and occupancy permits, and information on outstanding property tax and building charges.
  • Contract and payment. The terms of a reservation, deposit or sale agreement, what is being promised and how payment relates to the transfer.
  • Off-plan purchases. The developer, the project documents and the contract for a home that is not yet completed.

The legal rules and limits are explained in Buying property in Turkey as a foreigner.

The transfer and buying from abroad

We assist with preparing the transaction and attending the land registry transfer. If you intend to buy through a power of attorney, the wording and documents should be checked before you sign abroad. Translation and notarial requirements depend on the documents and the transaction.

Contact us before paying a deposit or signing an agreement where possible. If you have already signed or paid, explain what has happened and the current stage of the purchase.

What to include in your first enquiry

Please tell us where the property is, whether you have selected a property, whether it is completed or off-plan, and whether you have already signed anything or paid money. You can also say whether you will be in Turkey or wish to act from abroad.

Do not send passports, identity documents or sensitive financial records in the initial message. We will explain which documents are needed for the particular matter.

Language and communication

Clients may write in English or Turkish. Matters in English are handled by Av. Dr. Alihan Sağır; see the lawyers page. Official documents and procedures in Turkey are in Turkish, and we explain the steps in English.

Frequently asked questions

Can you review a purchase before I pay a deposit?

Yes. We can review the proposed contract and the property documents before you pay or sign. Tell us the stage of the transaction and where the property is located.

Can you assist with a property in Alanya or Kemer?

Our office is in Antalya city. We handle property matters across Antalya province, including Alanya, Manavgat, Side and Kemer.

Can I arrange a purchase from abroad?

A purchase can be handled through a properly prepared power of attorney. The powers and documents needed depend on the transaction. Contact us before signing the power of attorney abroad.

Can I write to you in English?

Yes. You can write in English or Turkish by email, WhatsApp or the contact form.
