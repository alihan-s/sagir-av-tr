Practice areas

Law of obligations and contracts

This area covers drafting, reviewing, performing and terminating contracts. The parties’ rights and obligations are examined in relation to breach, payment, default, tort and compensation. Depending on the dispute, the process may involve formal notices, negotiation, mediation and litigation.

Family law

Divorce and separation, maintenance, custody, contact with children and the division of matrimonial property are the principal subjects. Personal and financial consequences are considered together, with the child’s best interests guiding matters concerning children. Interim measures and protective remedies may also be relevant.

Criminal law

This area concerns legal proceedings during investigation, trial and enforcement of sentences. It includes defence rights and the participation of victims and complainants, statements and questioning, assessment of evidence and protective measures. Available remedies and procedural time limits depend on the stage of the case.

Employment law

Employment contracts, termination, wages and other employee claims, severance and notice compensation, reinstatement and determination of service periods fall within this area. The rights and obligations of employers and employees are assessed against contracts and employment records. Mediation and litigation are considered according to the type of dispute.

Inheritance law

This area covers inheritance certificates, shares in an estate, identification and distribution of estate assets, and dissolution of co-ownership. Wills and other testamentary dispositions are examined for validity, implementation and challenges. Reserved shares, reduction claims, rejection of inheritance and disputes among heirs depend on the circumstances.

Commercial and corporate law

Company formation, governance, shareholder relationships, share transfers and commercial contracts fall within this area. It includes general meeting and management resolutions, shareholder disputes, negotiable instruments and insurance claims. The nature of the business and the subject of the dispute determine the appropriate legal route.

Enforcement and bankruptcy law

Debt recovery, enforcement with or without a judgment, objections to payment orders, attachment and sale procedures are covered. Debtor and creditor rights are assessed against the basis of the debt, the enforcement route and the documents available. Objections, proceedings to overcome them and bankruptcy disputes are also examined.

Administrative law

This area concerns disputes arising from administrative decisions and actions. Zoning, expropriation, interference with property without expropriation and administrative restrictions on ownership are considered. Jurisdiction, conditions for administrative applications, annulment and compensation proceedings, and time limits depend on the measure concerned.

Startup law

From formation to investment discussions, this area addresses company structure, founder relationships and contractual arrangements. Founder and shareholder agreements, investment documents, confidentiality and service agreements are reviewed alongside intellectual property and personal data issues. Standard documents require assessment against the business model, ownership structure and Turkish law.

Urban transformation

This area covers risky-building assessments, notifications and objections, owners’ decisions, land shares and contractual relationships with contractors. Construction agreements in return for land shares, allocation of units, delivery terms and security are reviewed together. Administrative measures and private-law disputes may involve different remedies and procedural time limits.

These descriptions identify the office’s areas of practice; they do not confer a specialist title or promise a particular legal outcome.
