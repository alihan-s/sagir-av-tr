Working paper · Copyright, data protection and AI regulation (Türkiye / EU)

Software Rights, Cross-Border Data Transfers and EU AI Act Obligations for AI and SaaS Ventures in Türkiye

Av. Dr. Alihan Sağır · ORCID 0000-0002-3267-3838 · Version 2.0, 2 November 2026

An AI or SaaS venture may develop its own code, incorporate third-party software and models, and send customer data to external infrastructure. Each choice raises a distinct question about its ability to offer the resulting service. I examine how Turkish copyright rules establish the scope of protection and the venture’s authority to use software, how Article 9 KVKK governs cross-border personal-data flows, and how upstream licences condition distribution and network use. I then consider when the EU AI Act applies to a venture established in Türkiye and how its role determines the relevant duties and application dates. The analysis links those questions through the development and deployment of a single service: software rights, permission to process and transfer personal data, and regulatory responsibility each require their own legal basis.

Keywords: Artificial intelligence law; SaaS; Software copyright; Software licensing; KVKK Article 9; AGPLv3; EU AI Act
