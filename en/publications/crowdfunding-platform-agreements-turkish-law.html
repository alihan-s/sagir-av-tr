The Triangular Contractual Structure and Information-Form Liability in Equity- and Debt-Based Crowdfunding Under Turkish Law

Av. Dr. Alihan Sağır · ORCID 0000-0002-3267-3838 · Version 2.0, 2 November 2026

Turkish crowdfunding combines a regulated campaign process with private-law relationships among the platform, fundraising party and investors. This working paper distinguishes the exemptions in Capital Markets Law No. 6362 from the continuing obligations in Communiqué III-35/A.2. It separates application from company formation and issuance, information-form approval from signature liability, general pre-emptive-right rules from the special voting regime for dematerialised funded companies, and oversight in equity-based crowdfunding from oversight in debt-based crowdfunding. I develop the contractual analysis presented in my doctoral thesis, in which I characterise the platform agreement as a mixed innominate contract. The three-party structure is used as an analytical framework for separate relationships, without assuming that they form one legal contract.

Keywords: Crowdfunding; Platform agreement; Mixed contract; Information-form liability; Pre-emptive rights; Turkish private law
