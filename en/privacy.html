Privacy

Scope and contact

This notice describes data flows on sagir.av.tr and optional third-party services. Privacy enquiries concerning the site and correspondence may be addressed to Dr. Alihan Sağır at alihansagir@gmail.com or Gençlik Mah. Fevzi Çakmak Cad. 79/3 Muratpaşa / Antalya, Türkiye.

Contact form

The form contains name, email, optional subject and message fields. It does not send them to a server; it creates a draft in your email application. You must send the draft from that application. The site does not store entries in persistent browser storage. Data received by email may be processed to assess and answer your enquiry, on the applicable grounds of entering into or performing a contract, or establishing, exercising or protecting a right.

Direct email and telephone

Email and telephone links open the relevant application on your device. If you send a message there, its contents pass through your service and the recipient’s email provider. Correspondence with the office is handled to assess and respond to your enquiry. Messages sent to Gmail addresses also involve Google’s services and data-processing terms.

Google Maps

The map does not load automatically. Selecting “Show map” loads Google Maps and connects your browser to Google’s servers. Google may process your IP address, device and browser information under its own policy, use cookies or similar technologies, and process data abroad. Closing the map does not reverse data already transferred. The external Maps link also takes you to Google.

Cookies, tracking and technical logs

The site’s own code uses no advertising, visitor analytics, cookies or persistent browser storage. Fonts and images are served from site files. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses, request times and browser information to deliver and secure the service. Optional Google Maps and email services have their own processing practices.

Rights and retention

Requests under Turkish Law No. 6698, including access, correction and erasure where applicable, may be sent to the contact above. Correspondence is retained only as required for the enquiry, applicable legal obligations and protection of rights; no single period applies to every message. Email and map providers may process data abroad. The hosting provider’s retention and security policies apply to its technical data.
