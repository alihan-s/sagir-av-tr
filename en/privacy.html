Privacy

Scope and contact

This notice describes data flows on sagir.av.tr and optional third-party services. Privacy enquiries concerning the site and correspondence may be addressed to Av. Dr. Alihan Sağır at alihansagir@gmail.com or Gençlik Mah. Fevzi Çakmak Cad. 79/3 Muratpaşa / Antalya, Türkiye.

Contact form

The form contains name, email, optional subject and message fields. When you press send, the details go to a server function running on Cloudflare Workers, which forwards them to the office’s email address through the email delivery service Resend. The site does not store entries in persistent browser storage. The data are processed to assess and answer your enquiry. Under Turkish Law No. 6698 the legal ground is, depending on the case, that processing is directly related to entering into a contract, necessary to establish, exercise or protect a right, or necessary for the legitimate interest in answering the enquiry (Art. 5(2)(c), (e) and (f)). Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, the basis is steps taken at your request before entering into a contract and the same legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Using the form is optional; you can also contact the office by telephone or direct email.

Direct email, telephone and WhatsApp

Email and telephone links open the relevant application on your device. If you send a message there, its contents pass through your service and the recipient’s email provider. Correspondence with the office is handled to assess and respond to your enquiry. Messages sent to Gmail addresses also involve Google’s services and data-processing terms. The WhatsApp link opens WhatsApp on your device; messages sent through WhatsApp are subject to Meta’s terms and data-processing rules.

Google Maps

The map does not load automatically. Selecting “Show map” loads Google Maps and connects your browser to Google’s servers. Google may process your IP address, device and browser information under its own policy, use cookies or similar technologies, and process data abroad. Closing the map does not reverse data already transferred. The external Maps link also takes you to Google.

Cookies, tracking and technical logs

The site’s own code uses no advertising, visitor analytics, cookies or persistent browser storage. Fonts and images are served from site files. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; the contact form runs on Cloudflare Workers and messages are delivered through Resend. These providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, request times and browser information to deliver and secure the service. Optional Google Maps and email services have their own processing practices.

Rights and retention

You may exercise your rights under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 (including information, correction, erasure where applicable, and objection) using the contact details above; requests are answered within thirty days at the latest, and you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board. Where the GDPR applies, you have, subject to its conditions, the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country. Correspondence is retained only as required for the enquiry, applicable legal obligations and protection of rights; no single period applies to every message. Cloudflare, Resend and email and map providers may process data abroad, including in the United States. Transfers abroad to Cloudflare and Resend through the form are based on the explicit consent you give when sending the form, after being informed of the possible risks (Law No. 6698, Art. 9(6)(a); where the GDPR applies, Art. 49(1)(a)). In those countries personal data may not enjoy protection equivalent to that in Türkiye and the EU and may be subject to access by public authorities. You may withdraw your consent at any time using the contact details above; withdrawal does not affect earlier transfers. If you prefer not to consent, you can reach the office by telephone. The providers’ own retention and security policies apply to their technical data.
