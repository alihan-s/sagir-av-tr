Acting for the founders or for the investor

In an investment round, the investor's lawyer represents the investor and drafts the documents from that perspective. A lawyer instructed by the startup or its founders acts for the company or the founders. The work is to review the term sheet, the shareholders' agreement and the cap table mechanics before signing, so that terms that are difficult to change in later rounds - such as liquidation preferences, veto rights, anti-dilution formulas and founder leaver provisions - are clear in advance.

Where we act for a foreign angel investor, venture fund or corporate investor investing in a Turkish company, our work covers legal due diligence on the target's corporate records, software IP chain of title and material contracts, adapting the investment documents to Turkish law, and handling the power of attorney, share transfer or capital increase registration at the trade registry.

Common legal issues in Turkish startup rounds

Many obstacles in due diligence and investment closings come from a small number of recurring problems that can be addressed early.

  • Unassigned pre-incorporation or freelance code. If founders wrote software before the company was registered, or used outside contractors, FSEK Art. 18 on employee works does not apply. Under FSEK Art. 52, contracts and dispositions concerning economic rights must be in writing and must list each transferred right separately; for works not yet created, an undertaking under FSEK Art. 50/1 must be followed by a written dispositive assignment once the code exists.
  • Unadapted foreign SAFE and vesting templates. A US or UK SAFE or vesting agreement does not automatically issue or transfer shares in a Turkish company. Issuance of new shares requires a general assembly or board resolution, waiver or restriction of statutory pre-emption rights (TTK Art. 461) and registration at the trade registry.
  • Setting up as a Ltd. when an investment round is near. For companies established from 2024, minimum capital is TRY 50,000 for a Ltd. and TRY 250,000 for an A.Ş. (Presidential Decision No. 7887). In a Ltd., every share transfer requires a written contract with notarised signatures and, unless the articles provide otherwise, general assembly approval (TTK Art. 595). In an A.Ş., registered shares may be transferred without restriction unless the law or the articles provide otherwise (TTK Art. 490), and the company can issue preferred shares and use conditional capital increases.
  • Copyleft open-source components in SaaS products. Using AGPLv3 or GPL libraries in a network-delivered SaaS or AI product without reviewing licence obligations can raise source-code disclosure issues during investor due diligence.
  • Cross-border cloud and AI data transfers. Using foreign cloud infrastructure or external AI APIs involves cross-border personal data transfers under KVKK Art. 9, which require an appropriate legal transfer mechanism.

Antalya Teknokent and foreign founders

Startups in Antalya can incorporate either at an ordinary business address registered with the Antalya Trade Registry Directorate (which operates within the Antalya Chamber of Commerce and Industry, ATSO) or inside Antalya Teknokent at Akdeniz University, governed by the Technology Development Zones Law (No. 4691). Setting up inside the technopark is optional. Where a company applies to or operates in the zone, we review the lease and management company agreement alongside its corporate documents.

Foreign founders and foreign holding companies can own 100% of a Turkish limited or joint stock company without a Turkish partner or resident director. Most formation, shareholders' agreement and investment steps can be carried out from abroad under a power of attorney (vekaletname) notarised and apostilled in your country or signed at a Turkish consulate. For general subsidiary formation and foreign manager permits, see Company formation lawyers in Antalya and Foreign managers in a Turkish company.

Tax questions, including R&D and technopark tax incentives, belong to a Turkish certified public accountant (mali müşavir). We work alongside the company's accountant so that the legal structure and the tax treatment match.

Frequently asked questions

Should a startup in Turkey set up a Limited Company (Ltd.) or a Joint Stock Company (A.Ş.)?

If the venture expects angel or venture capital investment, an A.Ş. is usually preferred because registered shares may be transferred without restriction unless the law or the articles provide otherwise (TTK Art. 490), preferred shares can be issued (Art. 478) and conditional capital increases can be used for employee options (Arts. 463-472). In a Ltd., a share transfer requires a written contract with notarised signatures and, unless the articles state otherwise, general assembly approval (Art. 595). See Startup law in Antalya.

Are SAFE notes and foreign vesting clauses enforceable under Turkish law?

A SAFE or foreign vesting template is not self-executing at a Turkish trade registry. Under the Turkish Commercial Code, new shares are created through a capital increase resolution, waiver or restriction of pre-emption rights (TTK Art. 461) and registration. The investment and shareholders' agreements must therefore be adapted so that the contractual obligations fit the procedures of the TTK. See SAFE and convertible startup investments.

How are rights in software transferred from founders or developers to a Turkish startup?

For works created by employees in the course of their duties, the employer exercises the economic rights unless agreed otherwise (Law No. 5846, Art. 18/2). Code written by founders before the company was incorporated, or by outside contractors and freelancers, is not covered by Art. 18; it requires a written assignment that lists each transferred economic right separately (Art. 52).

Is it compulsory to set up a tech company inside Antalya Teknokent?

No. Technology Development Zones under Law No. 4691 are optional. A software or R&D company can incorporate outside the technopark at the Antalya Trade Registry, or apply to Antalya Teknokent if it wishes to operate in the zone.

Can foreign founders or investors complete incorporation and investment steps from abroad?

Most steps can be handled through a power of attorney (vekaletname) notarised and apostilled abroad or signed at a Turkish consulate. Some bank or immigration steps may still require a visit in person. We review or draft the power of attorney before you sign abroad.
