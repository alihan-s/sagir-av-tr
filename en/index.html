How the work is done from the UK

You do not need to be in Türkiye for most of the work. Instructions, documents and advice are in English. Documents are sent as scans, signed originals go by courier such as DHL or UPS, and a power of attorney signed before a UK notary with an apostille lets the property work be completed here without a trip. Questions UK clients ask

Do I need to travel to Türkiye?

Not for most property matters. A power of attorney signed in the UK and apostilled allows a named person to sign at the land registry and notary on your behalf. Some steps, such as a court hearing where your own testimony is needed, can require you to attend.

Can the title deed be checked before I pay a deposit?

Yes. The land registry record shows the owner, the share, mortgages and annotations, and the planning status of the plot or building. Do this check before any payment.

Does a British will cover my property in Türkiye?

A will made in the UK can be used in Türkiye, but Turkish law decides who inherits property in Türkiye and the reserved shares of close family. The heirs obtain a certificate of inheritance from a Turkish court, and the land registry then transfers the property to them.

Can I write to you in English?

Yes. You can write in English and receive the answer in English.
